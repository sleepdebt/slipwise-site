The problem

Incumbents sell you a bundle. You need three things.

Dockwa and DockMaster price slip booking, contracts, billing, POS, and fuel management as separate modules starting at $169–$249/month each. A marina that just wants slip booking, contracts, and payments ends up paying $500–700+/month for capability it's forced to buy in bundles.

The module stack
  • Slip booking module — separate subscription
  • Contracts & billing — another module, another fee
  • Fuel dock, POS, work orders — features you'll never use, priced in anyway
  • $500–700+/month once you add what you actually need
  • Sales calls and contracts to get started
